Privacy Policy – Rodmai Uploader

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Rodmai Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the "รอดไหม?" channel. Its only user is the channel owner.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and edit their details and playlists on the owner's own channel.
  • Facebook: permission to publish Reels to the owner's own Page.
  • TikTok: permission to send the owner's own videos to their own account.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the channel's own videos. The tool does not collect, sell, or share any personal data, and does not access data of other users or viewers.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page, Facebook Business Integrations settings, or TikTok app permissions.
